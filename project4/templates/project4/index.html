What the model is

A film is a feature vector x; a participant is a preference vector w; the utility of a film to them is U(x) = w · x. Eliciting preferences means estimating w from as few interactions as possible.

A pairwise choice is Bradley-Terry: P(i > j) = σ(w · (xi − xj)). A ranking of ten is read as a sequence of choices — favourite of ten, then of the remaining nine, and so on — which is the Plackett-Luce model. It reduces exactly to Bradley-Terry when there are two items, so both designs are fitted under one likelihood and the study compares the interfaces rather than two different models.

Features: {{ dataset.n_genres }} genres (multi-hot), {{ dataset.eras|length }} eras and {{ dataset.certificates|length }} certificate bands (one-hot), plus IMDb score, log vote count and runtime (standardised). Films need at least {{ dataset.min_votes }} votes to appear, so participants have some chance of recognising them.
